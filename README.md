# subsocial

A local-first mobile feed for Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, X, and YouTube.

Connect your accounts and read posts from your platforms in one chronological feed.
Saved posts and login sessions stay on your device. The app connects directly to platform websites
and has no server of its own.

## Usage

#### Connect platforms

Open the menu at the top of the feed and tap the globe icon next to a platform to sign in.
Close the browser to return to the feed. Connecting a new platform refreshes all connected platforms.

Use the eye icon next to a connected platform to show or hide its posts.

#### Feed

Scroll through posts, threads, images, and videos from your visible platforms.
Tap Show more to expand long posts, or tap an image to open it full screen.
Open an original post in the platform's app when installed, or in the built-in browser.

The feed refreshes automatically on the first app open each day. Pull down to refresh it manually.
Saved posts remain readable while new posts load. Open the feed menu to see which platforms are
still refreshing.

Your reading position is saved. When scrolling back up through the feed, use Back to top to return
to the newest posts.

#### Platform browser

Use the globe icon in the feed menu to browse a platform's website.
The browser toolbar also lets you open its installed app.

To disconnect a platform or switch accounts, use the logout button in the browser toolbar.
This deletes that platform's saved posts and login cookies from subsocial. Logging out of YouTube
also clears the Google sign-in cookies used by YouTube.

#### Export data

Tap the export icon at the top of the feed menu to share a backup of the feed database.
The backup includes saved posts and media metadata, but not login cookies or app preferences.

The app keeps the newest 500 feed groups per platform, including complete threads.
Older posts are removed as new ones arrive, so the saved feed is a recent cache rather than an archive.
