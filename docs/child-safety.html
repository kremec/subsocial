subsocial Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Our standards

subsocial prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual extortion, trafficking and any content or conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children. These standards apply to the use of subsocial, including content accessed through connected platforms.

Connected platforms

subsocial is an independent personal feed reader. It displays content from connected third-party platforms and does not operate those platforms or control their accounts. The original platforms handle reports about their posts and users through their own reporting tools. These tools can be accessed by opening the original post or platform.

This does not replace our responsibility to address child-safety concerns involving subsocial, including concerns about how it displays or retains content.

Reporting a concern

Report posts and users through the original platform. To report a child-safety concern involving subsocial, contact: klemen.remec@gmail.com.

Include the platform name, the original post or profile URL if available, and a short description of the concern. Do not download, attach or forward suspected child sexual abuse material. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

How we respond

We review child-safety reports and take appropriate action within our control in accordance with these standards and applicable law. This may include addressing app behavior that exposes prohibited material, preventing affected content from being displayed through subsocial, or restricting an affected integration where necessary. We cannot delete posts or suspend accounts on the original platforms.

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM accessible through subsocial, we act to address it and report it to the relevant regional or national authorities as required by applicable law. We cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.

Child-safety contact

subsocial's developer is responsible for handling reports received through the contact route above and serves as the designated child-safety point of contact for questions about these standards and our response practices. Personal information provided in reports is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

Updates

We may update these standards as subsocial or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
