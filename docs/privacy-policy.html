subsocial Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Who we are

subsocial is a personal feed reader that brings content from your connected social platforms into one chronological feed. The publisher responsible for subsocial is subbyte, based in Slovenia. In this policy, "we" refers to that publisher. For privacy questions or requests, email klemen.remec@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

When you connect Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, X or YouTube, subsocial loads that platform's website in an integrated browser. You sign in with the platform directly. The browser stores cookies and other website data on your device to maintain your session. subsocial checks session cookies to identify whether you are connected; it does not send your passwords or session cookies to a subsocial-operated server.

subsocial automatically reads content available through connected feeds when refreshing them. It processes and stores post text, author names and handles, post identifiers, links, timestamps, thread information and media references on your device to display your combined feed. This can include non-public content available to your signed-in account. Images, videos and website resources may also be cached on your device.

The app stores connection status and preferences locally. It checks whether supported platform apps can be opened so that links can open in the appropriate app. This check is not used to build an advertising profile.

subsocial does not require a separate subsocial account and does not upload your combined feed to a subsocial-operated server. We do not sell your personal information or use your feed to target advertisements.

Connections to other services

Local feed storage does not mean that the app operates without sending information over the internet. Loading feeds, signing in, opening posts and requesting media communicates directly with the relevant platforms and their service providers. They receive information such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested resources and applicable cookies. When you are signed in, they may associate these requests with your account. Their websites may use their own analytics, advertising and cookies.

The app uses Expo's update service to check for and download app updates. Expo receives network information such as your IP address and update-related technical information, such as your operating system, app runtime and update identifiers. Expo states that its update service may also collect randomized tokens to determine whether an update was downloaded. Your feed database and platform login cookies are not included in subsocial's update requests. See Expo's privacy policy.

Platform data practices are described in their policies: Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, X, and Google and YouTube. Opening another website or app subjects your interactions there to its own privacy practices.

Exports and support

If you choose to export your data, subsocial creates a copy of its feed database and opens your device's sharing interface. The export can contain saved posts, author information, links and connection metadata, including content from non-public feeds. It does not include the browser's login cookies. The destination you choose receives the exported file and handles it under its own policies.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use these to respond to your request, provide support and address security or legal matters. Email is handled through Google's email service. Please do not send passwords, session cookies or private feed exports unless specifically necessary for your request.

Retention and deletion

Saved feed content remains on your device until it is removed by the app's cache limits, you delete the platform's saved data, or you clear the app's storage. Retention is based on the feed's size rather than a fixed number of days. Cached posts may remain after the original post changes or is removed from its platform.

To remove a connected platform's saved feed data and login cookies, open that platform's browser in subsocial and use "Log out and delete data". This does not delete your account with the platform. Other website storage or media caches may remain.

The current export feature leaves a local export copy until the next export replaces it or the app's storage is cleared. Logging out of a platform does not remove that earlier export. To remove all local app data on Android, use your device's Settings to clear subsocial's storage. On iOS, delete the app rather than offloading it. Copies saved or shared outside the app must be deleted separately.

Depending on your device and backup settings, app data may be included in operating-system backups or device transfers. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups. Manage these through your device or backup provider.

We retain support correspondence only as long as necessary to handle the request and any related security, dispute or legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it. Other service providers retain information they receive according to their own policies. We cannot remotely access or erase a feed database held only on your device.

Security and international processing

Local app data is protected by your operating system's app isolation and device security. Connections to configured platform and update services use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; protect your device and exported files.

Platforms, Expo and email providers may process information outside Slovenia and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their privacy policies explain their locations and transfer safeguards. Keeping the feed database on your device does not prevent these network-related transfers.

Your privacy rights

Where we process personal data as a controller, we rely on providing the functionality you request where necessary to perform our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and maintaining security, and compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is required, it must be obtained separately; this policy itself does not constitute consent.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact us using the email above. Requests concerning data held by a connected platform should also be directed to that platform.

You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Slovenia's Information Commissioner. subsocial does not use your personal information to make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice or seek consent when required by law.
